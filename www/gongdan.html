01

政策概述 Overview

本隐私权政策适用于 Repair Work Orders（中文名称：维修工单助手，以下简称“本应用”）。本应用面向个体维修师傅和小型维修店，用于记录客户、设备、报价、维修过程、收款和保修信息。 This Privacy Policy applies to Repair Work Orders (Chinese name: 维修工单助手, referred to as “the App”). The App helps independent technicians and small repair shops record customers, devices, quotations, repair work, payments, and warranty information.

当前版本不要求用户注册账号，也不以广告、画像或跨应用跟踪为主要功能。我们按照“最小必要”和“本地优先”的原则处理信息。 The current version does not require an account and is not built around advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking. We follow data minimization and local-first principles.

02

我们处理哪些信息 Information we process

你可以自行决定在工单中填写哪些内容。为了实现应用功能，可能会在设备本地处理以下信息： You decide what to enter into a work order. To provide the App’s features, the following information may be processed locally on your device:

客户与商家资料 Customer and shop details

姓名、电话、微信号、服务地址、备注，以及你保存的门店或维修人员信息。 Names, phone numbers, WeChat IDs, service addresses, notes, and shop or technician details that you choose to save.

工单与交易记录 Work-order and payment records

设备信息、故障描述、维修项目、报价、成本、收款、保修和工单状态。 Device information, fault descriptions, repair items, quotations, costs, payments, warranty details, and work-order status.

照片与电子签名 Photos and signatures

你主动添加到工单中的维修照片和客户签名，用于维修记录与凭证生成。 Repair photos and customer signatures that you actively add to a work order for service records and document generation.

设置与备份 Settings and backups

显示设置、表单字段、项目模板，以及你主动导出的 JSON 或 CSV 文件。 Display settings, form fields, project templates, and JSON or CSV files that you choose to export.

我们不会为了提供基础工单功能，主动收集与业务无关的精确位置、通讯录、短信、麦克风内容或生物识别信息。 We do not proactively collect precise location, contacts, SMS, microphone content, or biometric information for the core work-order features.

03

本地存储与使用目的 Local storage and use

工单、客户、项目模板、照片索引、签名、收款记录和相关设置主要保存在应用的私有目录中，用于创建、编辑、查询、生成凭证、统计、备份和恢复。没有你的主动操作，这些业务数据不会上传到开发者的业务数据服务器。 Work orders, customers, project templates, photo indexes, signatures, payment records, and related settings are primarily stored in the App’s private storage for creating, editing, searching, generating documents, statistics, backup, and restore. These business data are not uploaded to the developer’s business data server without your action.

如果你卸载应用、清除应用数据或在应用内删除记录，本地数据可能会被移除。你此前导出的备份、分享文件或系统备份不受应用内删除操作控制，请一并管理这些副本。 Uninstalling the App, clearing its data, or deleting records in the App may remove local data. Backups, shared files, or system backups that you previously created are outside the App’s control and should be managed separately.

04

设备权限 Device permissions

本应用只在对应功能需要时请求权限。你可以拒绝权限；拒绝后，其他不依赖该权限的功能仍可继续使用。 The App requests permissions only when a related feature needs them. You may deny a permission; other features that do not depend on it can continue to work.

  • 相机：仅用于你主动拍摄维修前、维修中或维修后的照片。照片会添加到当前工单，并默认保存在设备上。 Camera: used only when you choose to take before, during, or after repair photos. Photos are added to the current work order and stored on the device by default.
  • 照片或文件选择：仅用于你主动选择图片、导入备份或选择导出位置。系统会按照你选择的文件和系统权限返回内容。 Photo or file picker: used only when you choose images, import a backup, or select an export location. The operating system returns only the files and access allowed by your selection.
  • 网络：仅在需要应用商店购买、恢复购买或验证专业版授权等功能时使用；离线工单功能不依赖网络。 Network: used when app-store purchasing, purchase restoration, or Pro entitlement verification is needed; offline work-order features do not depend on a network connection.

应用内购买与授权验证 In-app purchases and entitlement verification

如果你购买或恢复专业版，支付由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 等应用商店处理。本应用不会获取你的银行卡号、支付密码或完整支付账户信息；具体支付信息受相应商店的隐私政策约束。 If you purchase or restore Pro features, payment is handled by Apple App Store, Google Play, or the applicable app store. The App does not receive your card number, payment password, or full payment-account details; payment information is governed by the applicable store’s privacy policy.

为验证购买并在更换设备后恢复授权，应用可能向开发者运营的授权服务发送必要的技术信息，包括平台、商品 ID、交易标识、购买验证数据和授权状态。授权服务只处理购买与授权，不接收客户、工单、照片或签名内容。 To verify purchases and restore entitlement on another device, the App may send necessary technical information to the developer-operated entitlement service, including platform, product ID, transaction identifier, purchase-verification data, and entitlement status. The entitlement service handles purchases and access rights only; it does not receive customer, work-order, photo, or signature content.

专业版授权快照保存在设备的安全存储中，不会写入工单 JSON 备份，也不会通过工单数据同步。 The Pro entitlement snapshot is stored in the device’s secure storage. It is not included in a work-order JSON backup and is not synced through work-order data.

These actions are initiated by you. Once you send a file to a third party, that recipient’s privacy policy and security practices govern its use of the content.

本应用可能使用以下平台或服务来完成其功能： The App may use the following platforms or services to provide its features:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play：应用分发、支付和购买记录管理。 Apple App Store / Google Play: app distribution, payment processing, and purchase-record management.
  • 开发者授权服务：购买验证和专业版授权状态管理。生产环境可能使用 play.cosdk.com。 Developer entitlement service: purchase verification and Pro entitlement management. The production environment may use play.cosdk.com.
  • 系统分享与文件服务：由操作系统和你选择的目标应用完成文件传递。 System sharing and file services: file delivery is handled by the operating system and the destination app you select.

当前版本不提供云端工单同步，也不集成广告网络或第三方行为分析 SDK。 The current version does not provide cloud work-order synchronization and does not integrate advertising networks or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

保留、删除与安全 Retention, deletion, and security

本地业务数据通常会保留到你在应用内删除、清除应用数据或卸载应用为止。授权服务上的购买记录和授权事件会在为购买验证、恢复购买、欺诈防范、故障排查、财务记录或法律义务所需的期限内保留，之后会按照适用要求删除或匿名化。 Local business data generally remains until you delete it in the App, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Purchase records and entitlement events on the entitlement service are retained for as long as needed for purchase verification, restoration, fraud prevention, troubleshooting, accounting, or legal obligations, and are then deleted or anonymized as required.

我们采取合理的技术和组织措施保护信息，包括使用应用私有存储、平台安全存储和 HTTPS 传输（适用于生产授权服务）。但任何设备、网络或存储系统都不能保证绝对安全，请同时使用设备锁屏，并妥善保管导出的备份。 We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including app-private storage, platform secure storage, and HTTPS for the production entitlement service. No device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed absolutely secure, so please use a device lock and protect exported backups.
